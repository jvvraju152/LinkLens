LinkLens Link Check

Settings

An https:// address, or http:// on this computer. The default is the local server, http://127.0.0.1:5000. The extension can only talk to the server named here.

Links in emails often carry private tokens after the “?”. Leaving it out keeps them to yourself, but a few exact matches in the threat databases may be missed. The part after “#” is never sent.

What this extension does with your data

      
    
        
  • It checks only the links and pages you pick from the right-click menu. It does not watch your browsing.
    • 
        
  • It sends that one address (without the part after “#”) to your server. The link is never opened or fetched by the extension.
    • 
        
  • It stores your two settings above. The current result is held in memory until you close the browser, for at most an hour.
    • 
        
  • Your server decides what is sent on to threat databases and does not save these checks in scan history.
